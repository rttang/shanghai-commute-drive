Bank of China Tower, Shanghai

Shanghai, China

Scroll Down

The gate tower that represents the Pudong Financial Center

This office building serves as a gateway tower from Shanghai's new airport to downtown Shanghai. Located in the heart of the Pudong New Development District, it is adjacent to the Central Green Space and the Oriental Pearl TV Tower. Emphasis was placed on the view from the Shanghai Bund district across the Huangpu River, and while the building's exterior has distinctive features, care was taken to harmonize with the surrounding high-rise buildings.
ARCHITECT Shanghai Pudong International Finance Building Co., Ltd.
LOCATION Shanghai, China
SITE AREA 9,919.00 ㎡
TOTAL FLOOR AREA 116,945.00 ㎡
BUILDING HEIGHT GL+226.10 m
COMPLETION 2000
INFO Joint design: Shanghai Modern Design Group (Shanghai Architectural Design Research Institute), joint supervision: Shanghai Architectural Design Research Institute
